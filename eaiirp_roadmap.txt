Target: Production-oriented flagship project demonstrating Solution Architecture, Python, GenAI, RAG, Agentic AI, MCP, security and DevOps.

Estimated duration: 30 working days, approximately 3 hours daily.

Daily working approach
For each day, we'll follow this pattern:
- 30 minutes: Understand the real-world problem and architecture.
- 90 minutes: Implement step by step with commented code.
- 30 minutes: Test and debug.
- 30 minutes: Understand design decisions and prepare interview questions.
